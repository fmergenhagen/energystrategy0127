\section{Step 5 --- electricity demand overlay}
%% ------------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.96\textwidth}{
\textbf{Why (Step 5).}
PyPSA-DE spreads \emph{national} electricity demand by raster onto HV buses.
Landkreis Lüneburg then only gets the load that lands on the real UW (N01);
N02--N11 get nothing, and the annual total/shape are not the local meter truth.
We therefore \emph{overwrite} N01--N11 with a local overlay and shift the previous
N01 attribution into \texttt{DE-restAC} so Germany's total load is unchanged.
Heat (Heitkötter) is \textbf{not} part of this step --- that is Step 6.
}}

\vspace{0.8em}

\subsection{What / sources}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Annual:} Energiemonitor \texttt{Verbrauch} 2025 $\approx$\,\textbf{521\,GWh}.
  \item \textbf{Shape:} Avacon \texttt{load\_profile\_hourly\_norm.csv} (8760 shares, $\sum=1$),
        mapped by hour-of-year onto model timestamps (weather year 2013).
  \item \textbf{Spatial split:} population shares
        $\Rightarrow$ $L_i(t)=521\,\mathrm{GWh}\cdot w_i\cdot s(t)$.
  \item \textbf{Not Heitkötter} (heat only).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Code touch points (keep small)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \emph{New:} \texttt{modify\_electricity\_demand.py} + one rule in
        \texttt{modifications.smk} $\to$ \texttt{electricity\_demand-modified.nc}
  \item \emph{One switch:} \texttt{input\_electricity\_demand()} used by
        \texttt{compose} and \texttt{time\_aggregation} when \texttt{lueneburg.enable}
  \item Table: \texttt{data-lueneburg/Electricity/lueneburg\_el\_demand\_annual.csv}
\end{itemize}

\section{Step 6 --- heat demand + DH share}
%% ------------------------------------------------------------------

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.96\textwidth}{
\textbf{Why (Step 6).}
Electricity (Step 5) and heat are different carriers. Stock PyPSA-DE heat annuals
come from national totals $\times$ population and shapes from atlite HDD --- so
synthetic nodes N02--N11 get no heat, and the Landkreis total/shape are wrong.
Heitkötter gives a calibrated residential heat series for AGS\,3355; the local
Fernwärme register gives node DH shares. We overlay those and leave heating
\emph{technology stock} to a later step.
}}

\vspace{0.8em}

\subsection{What / sources}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Annual:} Heitkötter AGS\,3355 --- space $\approx$\,1104\,GWh + DHW $\approx$\,192\,GWh
        (all booked as residential; no services split in source).
  \item \textbf{Shape:} Heitkötter 15-min $\to$ hourly, hour-of-year onto model timestamps.
  \item \textbf{Spatial split:} population shares (same as electricity).
  \item \textbf{DH share:} \texttt{data-lueneburg/Heat/lueneburg\_dh\_share.csv} from Gemeinden
        \texttt{Fernwaerme\_Anteil} (mean per node); urban fraction floored to DH share.
  \item \textbf{Conserve} DE totals via \texttt{DE-restAC}.
  \item \textbf{Tiny stock fix:} \texttt{build\_heat\_demand} maps \texttt{N**AC}$\to$DE
        for heating efficiencies (\texttt{str[:2]} would yield \texttt{N0}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step 9 --- KPIs (drafted 2026-11-09)}

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.96\textwidth}{
\textbf{What is a KPI?}
A \emph{key performance indicator}: one of a small set of numbers that answer
the RQ. Not every model output.
}}

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.96\textwidth}{
\textbf{Why these KPIs / framing.}
\texttt{S0@2025} $=$ today; \texttt{S1@2040} $=$ no fossil heating.
Exogenous 521\,GWh applies to \emph{today} only. The S1 story is extra
electricity for heat + which generation/\textbf{storage} is built.
}}

\paragraph{KPI families}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{demand:} today exogenous el (521\,GWh); S1 electricity-for-heat (HP/RH);
        optional EV load (see below)
  \item \textbf{capacity:} installed / optimal / new MW on N01--N11 (RE, HP, boilers)
  \item \textbf{storage:} battery (+ others if present); \textbf{heat/TES}
        (water tanks / thermal stores --- \texttt{sector.tes: true} in stock)
  \item \textbf{cost / CO$_2$:} system supporting metrics
  \item \textbf{baselines:} Energiemonitor + MaStR
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{EVs}
Stock config has \texttt{sector.transport: true}; DE mobility shares electrify
strongly toward 2040 (\texttt{land\_transport\_ice\_share} falls, electric rises via
Aladin overwrite). So \textbf{yes, EVs play a role} in 2040 electricity demand by
default --- they are \emph{not} the RQ, but they confound a heat-only comparison.
Options: (a) keep and report EV load as its own KPI row; (b) freeze electric-share
at 2025 for a heat-only study.

\paragraph{Imports}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Abroad:} off --- \texttt{countries: ['DE']} and
        \texttt{sector.imports.enable: false} (no Stromnachbarn / H$_2$ imports in v1).
  \item \textbf{Landkreis $\leftarrow$ DE-rest:} \emph{yes, central.}
        N01--N11 exchange power with \texttt{DE-restAC} over the clustered lines.
        Net annual import (GWh) and peak import (MW) are KPIs for autarky /
        ``how much must the rest of DE cover when heat electrifies''.
  \item Fuels (gas/oil) into LG heat are implicit via carrier use until we add
        explicit fuel-balance KPIs.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Waste heat \& wind/PV potentials}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Why N01--N11 trade despite Landkreis totals:}
        Annual el/heat are Landkreis totals, but they are \emph{split} onto
        nodes (pop weights), MaStR stock is uneven, DH shares differ, and
        RE land differs by polygon. Heat does \emph{not} trade between
        Gemeinden; only electricity does --- over placeholder
        \texttt{LG-*} lines ($s_\mathrm{nom}=200$\,MW, adjacency matrix) and
        links to \texttt{DE-restAC}. Collapsing to one LG copperplate would
        kill intra-LG flows by construction; the 11 nodes exist so rural RE
        surplus can serve urban HP load.
  \item \textbf{How we get \texttt{p\_nom\_max}:}
        stock pipeline --- atlite cutout
        \texttt{de-2013-sarah3-era5} $\times$ exclusion/availability matrix
        $\times$ N01--N11 busshapes $\to$
        \texttt{build\_renewable\_profiles}
        ($p_\mathrm{nom,max}=\mathrm{MW/km}^2\times\mathrm{avail.}\times\mathrm{area}$
        summed per bus). Read from
        \texttt{resources/.../profile\_\{onwind,solar\}.nc} or
        \texttt{n.generators.p\_nom\_max}. MaStR overwrote \emph{existing}
        only; potentials stay stock. Optional later: tighter local land-use
        caps.
  \item \textbf{Waste / excess heat --- use Manz et al.\ for DH:}
        yes for stock geothermal (and other Manz sources if listed).
        Data: Fraunhofer Fordatis / Manz et al.\ 2024, mapped LAU $\to$
        onshore regions (= our N** polygons) in
        \texttt{build\_geothermal\_heat\_potential}. Enable with
\begin{lstlisting}
sector:
  heat_pump_sources:
    urban central: [air, geothermal]
\end{lstlisting}
        Default is air-only. Separate: PtX process waste heat
        (\texttt{use\_*\_waste\_heat} flags) only if those plants sit on the
        node; Hotmaps industrial sites for plant-level excess heat.
        No custom LG industrial inventory yet --- Manz geothermal is the
        clean v1 path for DH excess/renewable heat.
\end{itemize}
